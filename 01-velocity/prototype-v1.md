## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** it showed onboarding progress in the past 30 days, recommended next steps for onboarding and a help section
- **What confused them:** I was not very clear what we were trying to measure or what the outcome was supposed to be
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** it seems to be testing an assumption that if there are recommended steps customer churn would reduce
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** I think it was not very clear what was being measured, what the assumotions or hypothesis is
